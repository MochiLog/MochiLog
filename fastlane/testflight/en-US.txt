4.1.1

Fixed an issue that could close MochiLog when switching to another app after an update.

Please check:
• Update over an earlier version and confirm that your existing records appear.
• Open Home, record details and sharing, then switch to another app and return.
• Import analytics logs from the share menu or Files and check the results and saved records.
• Close and reopen MochiLog and confirm that your records remain available.

Please test on iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo.
